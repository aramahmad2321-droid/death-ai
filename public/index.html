✦ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Zana (زانا) is a Kurdish word meaning "the wise one" or "the knowledgeable one." It's a common name in Kurdish culture for someone renowned for their wisdom and learning — exactly the kind of AI assistant we set out to build.
Zana is powered by a state-of-the-art large language model. The specific underlying model can be configured by the platform administrator — making Zana compatible with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and other providers through a unified interface.
Absolutely. Zana AI uses JWT authentication, HTTPS encryption, rate limiting, XSS protection, and MongoDB for secure data storage. Your conversations are only visible to you and are never shared with third parties.
Kurdish Sorani is a first-class language in Zana. The interface fully supports RTL text, the fonts are optimized for Arabic script, and Zana's AI personality is specifically tuned to respond naturally in Sorani — understanding cultural context and regional idioms.
Zana is a Progressive Web App (PWA) which means you can install it on your device and it will load from cache even without internet. However, generating AI responses requires an active internet connection to reach the AI servers.
In any conversation, click the export icon in the top toolbar. You can download your chat as a plain TXT file or a professionally formatted PDF with Zana AI branding — perfect for keeping records or sharing with colleagues.
